FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

If you don't see what you're looking for, just call us — (337) 478-8481.

What documents do I need to bring to my appointment?
Bring your photo ID, Social Security cards for you and any dependents, all W-2s and 1099s, last year's tax return, and any records of deductible expenses. Not sure what applies to you? Call us and we'll walk you through it.
Do you offer mobile tax preparation?
Yes. We offer full-service mobile tax preparation and will come to your home or office if an in-office visit doesn't fit your schedule.
Do you prepare taxes for LLCs, S-corps, partnerships, and nonprofits?
Yes. We handle tax preparation for sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships, S-corporations, and nonprofits, in addition to individual returns.
Can you help if I'm behind on filing or need to amend a past return?
Yes. We prepare prior-year returns and amendments to get you caught up and compliant.
What are your office hours?
We're open Monday through Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and closed Friday through Sunday.
Do you offer bookkeeping and accounting services year-round?
Yes. Beyond tax season, we offer general accounting and bookkeeping support to keep your business's records organized and tax-ready all year.
How do I get started?
Call (337) 478-8481 or email wise.account@yahoo.com to schedule your appointment. We'll confirm a time and let you know exactly what to bring.
